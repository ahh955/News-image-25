One image summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026's world news: war at sea in the
Strait of Hormuz, Asia's market rout on fresh US tariff fears, and Shenzhou-24
carrying the first foreign astronaut to China's Tiangong station — plus 14 more
verified stories from the day, a world map with numbered dots, and a DATE CHECK
box. Every story happened, or was first reported, on 25 November 2026; nothing
is from before 13 November 2026. Claims are labelled CLAIM, forecasts FORECAST.

## How it works

`main.ts` copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to
`/output/entry.png`. The output is byte-identical on every run.
